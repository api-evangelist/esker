# Esker

> Esker provides AI-powered automation solutions for the Office of the CFO, helping businesses optimize Source-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash, e-invoicing, working capital and cashflow-related processes. Esker solutions are designed for B2B organizations seeking to automate finance, procurement, customer service and accounts receivable operations with secure cloud technology and AI.
